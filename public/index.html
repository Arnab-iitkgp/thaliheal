My log
How the numbers work. The AI only names dishes and estimates portions with a range. Nutrients come from the app's food table: per 100 g values calculated from IFCT 2017 ingredients with standard Indian recipes, plus typical values for fast food. Oil, ghee and butter are separate because a camera cannot see them.
Confidence. High, Medium or Low combines how sure the AI is about the dish with how good the table data is. The kcal range covers the portion range and the oil uncertainty. Estimates for a prototype, not medical advice.
